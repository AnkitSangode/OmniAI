# OmniAI — AI Governance and Acceptable Use Policy

## 1. Purpose

OmniAI provides AI-powered text, image, audio and video processing capabilities. This policy establishes responsible-use expectations and explains how user data is handled.

## 2. Acceptable Use

Users must not use OmniAI to:
- Generate or distribute illegal, abusive, threatening or harmful content.
- Create deceptive, fraudulent or impersonation content.
- Violate another person's privacy, intellectual property or consent.
- Upload malicious files or attempt to compromise the platform.
- Bypass safety controls or misuse AI-generated outputs.

Users are responsible for the prompts, files and content they submit and for reviewing generated outputs before using or distributing them.

## 3. Data Handling and Privacy

- User authentication and application records are stored in the platform database.
- Uploaded and generated media are processed by the relevant AI services.
- Prompts and generated responses may be processed by configured model providers.
- Operational metrics such as request status, model usage, latency and estimated cost may be recorded for analytics and debugging.
- Sensitive credentials and API keys must never be included in prompts or committed to source control.
- Access to user data must be restricted to authorized application operations.

OmniAI should not be used to submit highly sensitive personal, financial, medical or confidential information.

## 4. AI Safety and Content Moderation

OmniAI uses configured safety mechanisms, including Llama Guard for text moderation and image safety checks.

Requests or generated content identified as unsafe may be blocked.

Safety systems are not guaranteed to identify every harmful or inappropriate output. Users must exercise judgment and review generated content.

## 5. Misuse Reporting

Users can report suspected misuse, unsafe outputs, privacy concerns or security issues through the project maintainer's designated support or contact channel.

Reports should include:
- A description of the issue.
- The affected feature.
- The approximate date and time.
- Relevant request identifiers, if available.

Reports should not contain passwords, API keys or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

## 6. Enforcement

Access may be restricted or suspended when misuse, security violations or repeated attempts to bypass safety controls are identified.

## 7. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as OmniAI evolves. Continued use of the platform is subject to the latest published version.

---

**Note:** OmniAI is an experimental AI platform. Generated content may be inaccurate, incomplete or unsuitable for certain purposes. Users remain responsible for evaluating outputs before relying on them.
